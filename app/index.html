01 / 05 · The problem

Most credit scores read last year.

Companies file their annual accounts with AJPES by 31 March. In September you are judging a customer on figures that ended nine months ago, and one in twelve companies is still on the year before. The next accounts arrive in spring 2027.

Age of the newest accounts
9–21months

02 / 05 · Live payments

Rok reads the invoices.

Suppliers share their e-invoices with Rok. Each one shows when it was due and when it was paid, so every week we see how late each company pays. From 1 January 2028 every B2B invoice in Slovenia has to be electronic.

03 / 05 · The grade

One number you can price.

Every company gets a calibrated probability of default for the next 12 months and a grade from A+ to E. Its dossier lists the reasons, from a blocked account to a director linked to past bankruptcies.
