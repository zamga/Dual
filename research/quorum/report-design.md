**More detail on the notable ones**

- **Jeton.**
  - The site combines "serious finance flow" with scroll-based movement and a layout that morphs from desktop to mobile.
  - A Rive animation is indexed to scroll position.
  - The whole narrative is one claim: "one app for all needs."
  - The same project also won CSSDA Website of the Day, FWA Site of the Day, and two Gold awards at Portugal's CCP 2025 (Best Website; Craft Design UI).
  - The rebrand covered the website and the product UI together.
  - Sources: https://www.awwwards.com/case-study-jeton-by-burocratik.html · https://www.jeton.com/newsroom/wins-two-gold-awards-for-design-2025
- **Tresmares Capital.**
  - The concept comes from the name: the Pico Tres Mares mountain is rebuilt in 3D as the intro.
  - Abstract forms and natural elements stand in for the firm's financial products.
  - Navigation is organised around three questions (who they are, what they do, where they operate), so each visitor type finds its own path.
  - It includes an investor portal with legal documents and positions.
  - Source: https://dgrees.studio/works/tresmares-capital/
- **Shopify Live Globe.**
  - It shows 100% live data, rendered in real time.
  - A dot-matrix display shader (128×32 frames from a merged spritesheet, dot effect, bloom post-processing) shows the live sales numbers.
  - The team switched from Rapier 3D to Rapier 2D physics because the scene "stopped feeling good on older mobile devices." That is a direct lesson in performance-first choices.
  - Source: https://shopify.engineering/2025-bfcm-live-globe

## 3. What the best-designed investing products do

- **Robinhood.**
  - *Brand:* Porto Rocha rebrand, October 2024. The typefaces are a custom sans, **Robinhood Phonic** ("delicate ink-traps"), with a serif, **Martina Plantijn**, for headlines. The palette is black, white and "mature neutrals" plus one accent, **Robin Neon** (an electric yellow-green). Reviewers say it reads "more like a magazine than a brokerage." The 2025 Robinhood Gold work added "sophistication and dimensionality." https://www.underconsideration.com/brandnew/archives/new_logo_and_identity_for_robinhood_by_porto_rocha.php · https://www.portorocha.com/robinhood
  - *Robinhood Legend (desktop trading):* dark mode, modular drag-and-drop panes, up to 8 charts, trading directly from the chart, preset layouts, sub-second data updates. https://www.finder.com/investments/robinhood-legend
  - *Cortex Digests (AI summaries):* plain-English explanations of price moves on the stock page. Portfolio digests show the top return driver, top mover, upcoming earnings and splits, and a comparison against the S&P 500. There is a public **methodology page**. Robinhood reports that 95% of US users surveyed "love" it. https://newsroom.aboutrobinhood.com/digests-by-robinhood-cortex-uk/ · https://www.robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex-digests-methodology
- **Public.com:** its **Alpha** AI assistant (GPT-4) is on every asset page. Since December 2024 it has also been a standalone app that sends **notifications explaining why a stock is moving**. That is the closest existing analogue to our SMS alerts. https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/alpha-a-new-ai-watchlist-app-launches-to-give-individual-investors-real-time-insights-on-the-markets-302330059.html
- **Composer:** users describe a strategy in plain language and AI builds it. Backtests run instantly (data back to 2007) and show the equity curve, drawdown, Sharpe ratio, CAGR, max drawdown, and a comparison line against SPY. https://alpaca.markets/blog/how-composer-is-redefining-algorithmic-trading-with-their-no-code-platform/ · https://alphagaindaily.com/en/blog/composer-ai-trading-review
- **Koyfin:** resizable, draggable widgets; heatmaps and matrices; fundamentals overlaid on price charts. https://www.koyfin.com/features/
- **Stripe:**
  - A WebGL mesh gradient rendered with its own "minigl" library.
  - The typeface is Klim's Söhne (variable) with the ss01 stylistic set turned on everywhere.

## 4. How scoring works, and what jurors penalise

**Awwwards scoring:** Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%. Details: https://www.awwwards.com/about-evaluation/ · https://www.hontran.dev/blog/awwwards-judging-criteria
- At least 18 jurors score each site, and the 3 scores furthest from the average are dropped.
- Voting lasts 5 days.
- An Honorable Mention needs a jury score of 6.5 or more (per hontran.dev).
- Every SOTD winner goes to a developer jury. A score above 7 earns the Developer Award, which checks performance, SEO, accessibility, cross-browser behaviour and markup. https://www.awwwards.com/developer-award/
- The Mobile Excellence badge needs 75/100 or more. https://www.awwwards.com/mobile-award/
- Finance SOTDs in this research scored between **7.3 and 7.6**.

**What loses points:**
- Stock photography, generic grid layouts, design tokens that differ between pages, trends used without purpose. Core Web Vitals count toward usability. https://www.utsubo.com/blog/award-winning-website-design-guide
- A 3D hero that drops to 18 fps on a mid-range Android, or a 9 MB page that takes 6 seconds to paint, "will not win." A desktop masterpiece that breaks on iPhone "loses immediately." Launching and submitting in the same week means jurors see live bugs. https://www.hontran.dev/blog/best-award-winning-websites-2026
- Lorem ipsum or stock content (content is only 10% of the score but drags down every criterion). Template-built sites are not accepted. A site has 3 months after approval to win SOTD. https://www.elias.studio/en/blog/post/10-conseils-pour-gagner-un-site-of-the-day-sotd-sur-awwwards
- Scrolljacking harms user control and discoverability. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolljacking-101/
- Accessibility: Sites of the Month are "rarely navigable with a keyboard", and the main jury does not seem to score accessibility. The developer jury does. https://trost.codes/posts/accessible-awwwards/

**Cost:** award-level sites typically cost $30K–$200K+ and take 8–24 weeks (utsubo).

## 5. Patterns that recur in the winners

1. **Two-colour palettes: near-black and off-white plus one saturated accent.** Moto `#0B0C07`/`#E1E5E5`, EverSwap `#fbfff4`/`#203727`, Tresmares `#D81413`/`#fff`, Runway `#192227`/`#F9A600`, Into The Amazon `#FFD800`/`#111111`, Jeton `#F73B20` alone.
2. **One 3D object that carries the brand idea**, not decoration: Moto's card, Tresmares' mountain, EverSwap's scene.
3. **The Developer Award comes with the win.** Moto, EverSwap, Tresmares, Jeton and Shopify Globe all won it with SOTD. Top finance winners are engineered, not just styled.
4. **GSAP is everywhere.** 3D uses Three.js or OGL. Webflow is enough to win the Developer Award (Moto). Lenis and Rive handle scroll-driven storytelling (Jeton).
5. **Real data as the spectacle** (Shopify Live Globe) and **the product shown as the demo** (Stripe, Linear).
6. **Serious finance flows wrapped in expressive motion**, with navigation built around audience paths (Tresmares' investor portal).

## Implications for our build

1. **Target SOTD plus Developer Award plus Mobile Excellence, not Site of the Month.** No finance site won Site of the Month in 2025–2026, and finance SOTDs landed at 7.3–7.6. Budget 70% of the effort for Design and Usability, which is 70% of the score.
2. **Palette:** one near-black (around `#0A0B0C`), one off-white (around `#E8EAE6`), and one "signal" accent used *only* when a high-conviction pick fires: in the UI, the SMS mockup and the favicon badge. Red and green are for gains and losses only, always paired with a +/− sign and an arrow for colour-blind users.
3. **Type:** a licensed editorial serif for headlines, a grotesque sans for UI, and a mono for tickers and timestamps. Turn on tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) everywhere numbers appear. Do not use Söhne or Inter Display as the headline face; they read as Stripe and Linear.
4. **Hero concept (one object, real data):** render the stock universe as a live field of points, one per stock, coloured by model score. When the models agree, the points converge into a single point that becomes the SMS arriving on a phone. Use real, delayed data, never placeholders. This combines the Shopify Globe idea (real data as spectacle) with Moto's approach of building the whole page around one hero object.
5. **The SMS moment:** a Jeton-style animation where the desktop view morphs into a phone as you scroll, using Rive indexed to scroll position. Show a real past alert with its timestamp and the outcome afterwards.
6. **Trust signals above the fold:** a full track record including losing picks, each with timestamp, entry price and outcome versus a benchmark (S&P 500 or STOXX 600). A public **methodology page**, like Robinhood's Cortex methodology. A risk disclaimer next to every call to action. (The exact EU/Slovenian disclosure wording belongs to the compliance workstream; I did not verify it here.)
7. **Ungated sandbox of the research data on the marketing site:** a score explorer with factor-breakdown bars. Metrics copy Composer's set: CAGR, max drawdown, Sharpe, hit rate, and an equity curve against the benchmark. Use Koyfin-style draggable widgets in the logged-in dashboard, and Legend-style dark modular panes for power users.
8. **AI explanations:** each pick ships with a plain-English "why", like Cortex Digests or Public Alpha. Structure it as top drivers, risks, the next catalyst date and the benchmark. The same text feeds the SMS (short form) and the web page (long form).
9. **Performance and accessibility budgets:**
   - LCP under 2.5 s on a mid-range Android and 60 fps; the 3D scene falls back to a 2D canvas or static image on weak GPUs (the Shopify Rapier lesson).
   - Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
   - No scrolljacking: native scroll, with Lenis applied lightly at most.
   - Full keyboard navigation and WCAG AA contrast, because the developer jury scores accessibility.
10. **Stack:** Next.js (or Nuxt, as Jeton used), GSAP, Three.js or OGL (via React Three Fiber if we use React), Lenis, Rive, Sanity CMS, Vercel. All of these appear in the winners above.
11. **Submission plan:**
    - Launch, fix bugs for two to three weeks, then submit to Awwwards (within the 3-month window).
    - Also submit to CSSDA (Website of the Day needs above 8.0) and The FWA.
    - Enter the Webby "Fintech, Financial Services & Banking" website category in the next cycle (deadlines UNVERIFIED).
    - Real content only at submission time.

    - Plan for $30K–$200K+ and 8–24 weeks.
